# Public profile display defaults

These local assets are display fallbacks only. Their URLs must not be saved to profile records.

- `grower-profile-banner.webp`: generated orchard photograph, 1600 × 500 pixels.
- `buyer-profile-banner.webp`: generated fruit distribution warehouse photograph, 1600 × 500 pixels.
- `grower-profile-logo.svg`: original circular orchard icon, 192 × 192 view box.
- `buyer-profile-logo.svg`: original circular fruit business icon, 192 × 192 view box.

Both banners were created with the built-in image generation tool and optimized locally with Sharp (WebP quality 80). The circular logos are authored SVGs. The images intentionally contain no profile identity, text, or verification marks; those belong to the interface.

## Generation prompts

### Grower

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: default grower business profile banner for a professional fruit trading website. Primary request: a beautiful working fruit orchard with orderly lush green apple trees bearing ripe red and green fruit, a cultivated path and subtle distant hills. Professional natural editorial photography, authentic agricultural setting, calm trustworthy appearance. Wide panoramic composition approximately 16:5; keep recognizable fruit trees within the central horizontal band so the image works as a short cover banner. Soft morning daylight, natural rich green colors, tasteful contrast and true-to-life textures. No people, no words, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no badges, no UI. Full-bleed scene, no borders.

### Buyer

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: default buyer business profile banner for a professional fruit trading website. Primary request: a clean organized wholesale fruit market and distribution warehouse, neat wooden and green reusable produce crates filled with fresh red apples, green apples and oranges along the foreground, orderly open warehouse aisles behind them. Professional natural editorial commercial photography, authentic fruit trading environment, calm trustworthy appearance. Wide panoramic composition approximately 16:5; keep recognizable produce crates and warehouse structure within the central horizontal band so the image works as a short cover banner. Soft daylight through a high warehouse roof, subtle forest green accents, natural fruit colors, realistic textures, uncluttered. No people, no readable words or lettering, no signs, no brands, no logos, no watermark, no badges, no UI. Full-bleed scene, no borders.
