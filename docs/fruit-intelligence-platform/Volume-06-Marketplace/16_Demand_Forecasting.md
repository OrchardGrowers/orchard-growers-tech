# 1. Purpose

The Demand Forecasting framework estimates future marketplace demand using historical marketplace activity, seasonal patterns, inspection data, and verified platform events.

Its purpose is to help Growers, Buyers, logistics partners, and platform administrators make informed operational decisions.

Forecasts are advisory and should not be interpreted as guarantees.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Demand Forecasting framework shall:

- estimate future demand
- identify demand trends
- support harvest planning
- improve inventory planning
- support logistics preparation
- assist marketplace intelligence
- improve future AI models

---

# 3. Forecasting Pipeline

```text
Marketplace Events
        ↓
Historical Data
        ↓
Seasonal Analysis
        ↓
Regional Analysis
        ↓
Demand Modelling
        ↓
Confidence Evaluation
        ↓
Forecast Generation
        ↓
Marketplace Dashboard
```

# 4. Data Sources

Demand forecasting may use:

- Fruit Lots
- Buyer Offers
- Completed Deals
- Seasonal production
- Variety information
- Regional activity
- Inspection Reports
- Logistics history
- Marketplace participation
- Historical demand
- Public market indicators (where available)

# 5. Forecast Dimensions

Forecasts may be generated by:

- fruit
- variety
- region
- district
- state
- season
- marketplace segment
- buyer category

Additional dimensions may be introduced over time.

---

# 6. Forecast Outputs

Forecast outputs may include:

- expected demand level
- expected demand trend
- confidence score
- expected peak periods
- expected low-demand periods
- forecast horizon
- supporting observations

---

# 7. Confidence

Forecast confidence may consider:

- historical data volume
- seasonal consistency
- data quality
- marketplace participation
- regional coverage
- model performance

Lower confidence should be clearly indicated.

# 8. Human Interpretation

Forecasts are designed to support decisions.

Users should consider additional factors such as:

- weather
- local conditions
- government policy
- transportation
- export restrictions
- unexpected events

Human judgement remains essential.
