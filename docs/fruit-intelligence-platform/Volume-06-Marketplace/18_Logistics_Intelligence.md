---

# 1. Purpose

The Logistics Intelligence framework provides analytical insights to improve the planning, execution, monitoring, and optimization of fruit transportation throughout the marketplace.

Its purpose is to help Growers, Buyers, logistics partners, and platform administrators coordinate efficient and reliable movement of Fruit Lots.

Logistics Intelligence supports operational decisions.

It does not replace logistics providers or contractual agreements.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Logistics Intelligence framework shall:

- improve transportation planning
- reduce delivery delays
- support route optimization
- improve shipment visibility
- assist logistics partners
- support marketplace operations
- provide explainable operational insights

---

# 4. Data Sources

Logistics Intelligence may use:

- Fruit Lots
- pickup requests
- delivery schedules
- shipment events
- GPS location (where available)
- driver updates
- vehicle information
- delivery confirmations
- buyer acknowledgements
- logistics feedback

---

# 5. Operational Insights

The platform may generate:

- estimated pickup readiness
- estimated delivery time
- route efficiency
- transit duration
- shipment completion rate
- delay indicators
- logistics performance trends

These insights are advisory.

---

# 6. Performance Metrics

Operational metrics may include:

- pickup time
- loading duration
- travel duration
- unloading duration
- delivery success rate
- average transit time
- delay frequency
- route efficiency

---

# 7. Risk Indicators

Potential logistics risks include:

- weather disruption
- traffic congestion
- vehicle breakdown
- delayed pickup
- delayed delivery
- incomplete shipment information
- communication failures

Risk indicators should help users prepare rather than predict certainty.

---

# 8. AI Integration

Logistics Intelligence may integrate with:

- Marketplace Intelligence
- Demand Forecasting
- Price Intelligence
- AI Recommendation Engine
- Business Analytics

Each module should remain independently versioned.

---

# 9. Human Decision Making

The platform may recommend logistics actions.

Final operational decisions remain with:

- Growers
- Buyers
- Logistics Partners
- Platform Administrators

The platform should not automatically dispatch shipments unless explicitly configured by future platform policies.
