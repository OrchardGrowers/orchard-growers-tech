# 1. Purpose

The Business Intelligence framework transforms operational and marketplace data into actionable insights for strategic, tactical, and operational decision-making.

Its purpose is to help platform stakeholders understand performance, identify opportunities, monitor growth, and support continuous improvement.

Business Intelligence provides analytical insights.

It does not replace executive judgement.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Business Intelligence framework shall:

- monitor platform performance
- support strategic planning
- improve operational visibility
- identify business trends
- measure platform growth
- support data-driven decisions
- enable executive reporting

---
