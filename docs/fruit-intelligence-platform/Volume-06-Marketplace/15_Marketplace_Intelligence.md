**Platform:** eFruitMandi.live

**Company:** Orchard Growers Private Limited

**Document ID:** DFIP-V06-D15

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-17

---

# 1. Purpose

Marketplace Intelligence transforms marketplace activity into structured insights that improve transparency, operational efficiency, and future AI capabilities.

It combines marketplace data with Fruit Intelligence while keeping commercial decisions under user control.

Marketplace Intelligence supports decision-making.

It does not replace the marketplace.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Marketplace Intelligence framework shall:

- understand marketplace activity
- identify trends
- support growers
- support buyers
- improve transparency
- support analytics
- enable future AI services

---

# 3. Data Sources

Marketplace Intelligence may use information from:

- Fruit Lots
- Buyer Offers
- Deal confirmations
- Inspection Reports
- Packing information
- Logistics events
- Delivery outcomes
- Settlement history
- Marketplace feedback
- User activity
- Seasonal trends
