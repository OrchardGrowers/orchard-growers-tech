# AI Assisted Decision Framework

**Project:** Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform (DFIP)

**Platform:** eFruitMandi.live

**Company:** Orchard Growers Private Limited

**Document ID:** DFIP-V05-D14

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-17

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how Artificial Intelligence assists decision-making within the Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform.

AI provides recommendations, observations, predictions, and risk indicators to support users. Final commercial and operational decisions remain under human control unless explicitly authorized by future platform policies.

---

# 2. Objectives

The AI Assisted Decision Framework shall:

- support informed decisions
- improve operational efficiency
- preserve human authority
- provide explainable recommendations
- reduce manual effort
- maintain traceability
- support continuous learning

---

# 3. Decision Flow

```text
Platform Data
        ↓
AI Analysis
        ↓
Observation Generation
        ↓
Confidence Evaluation
        ↓
Recommendation Engine
        ↓
Human Review
        ↓
Final Decision
        ↓
Outcome Recording
        ↓
Future Learning
```

---

# 4. Decision Categories

AI may assist with:

- fruit inspection
- packing recommendations
- quality observations
- marketplace insights
- logistics planning
- pricing analytics
- demand forecasting
- inventory analysis
- operational alerts

Support for additional categories may be introduced over time.

---

# 5. Recommendation Types

Recommendations may include:

- informational
- advisory
- warning
- optimization
- anomaly notification
- confidence-based suggestions

Recommendations should clearly indicate their purpose.

---

# 6. Human Authority

Unless explicitly defined by platform policy:

- AI does not approve transactions
- AI does not reject Fruit Lots
- AI does not authorize payments
- AI does not replace inspectors
- AI does not replace Grower declarations
- AI does not replace Buyer acceptance

Human users remain responsible for final decisions.

---

# 7. Confidence and Explainability

Every AI recommendation should include, where practical:

- confidence score
- contributing observations
- supporting evidence
- known limitations
- applicable model version

Recommendations should be understandable by users.

---

# 8. Feedback Loop

Users may provide feedback such as:

- accepted recommendation
- rejected recommendation
- corrected observation
- additional comments

Feedback should support future model improvements while preserving historical records.

---

# 9. Decision Traceability

Each AI-assisted decision should retain:

- recommendation ID
- model version
- timestamp
- input references
- human action taken
- outcome status

Decision history should remain auditable.

---

# 10. Risk Management

Potential risks include:

- incomplete data
- inaccurate predictions
- low-confidence observations
- changing environmental conditions
- unsupported fruit varieties
- hardware limitations

Risk indicators should accompany recommendations when appropriate.

---

# 11. Future Evolution

Future versions may include:

- multi-model recommendations
- personalized decision support
- predictive workflows
- autonomous workflow assistance
- collaborative human-AI review

All future capabilities should remain governed by the AI Governance framework.

---

# 12. Design Principles

The AI Assisted Decision Framework should remain:

- explainable
- traceable
- human-centric
- modular
- scalable
- auditable
- secure
- backward compatible
