# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term technology roadmap for the Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform.

The roadmap describes the planned evolution of software, artificial intelligence, hardware, marketplace intelligence, and industrial inspection capabilities while preserving backward compatibility and platform stability.

The roadmap is directional rather than contractual.

---

# 2. Objectives

The roadmap shall:

- define long-term direction
- prioritize platform evolution
- support phased execution
- reduce implementation risk
- guide investment decisions
- align research with product development
- support global scalability

---

# 3. Technology Evolution

```text
Digital Marketplace
        ↓
Inspection Platform
        ↓
Fruit Intelligence
        ↓
AI Platform
        ↓
Dedicated Scanner
        ↓
Industrial Inspection
        ↓
Global Fruit Intelligence Network
```

Each stage builds upon capabilities introduced in earlier phases.

---

# 4. Phase 1 — Digital Marketplace

Primary objectives:

- Grower onboarding
- Buyer onboarding
- Fruit Lots
- Offer management
- Deal confirmation
- Logistics support
- Payment integration
- Marketplace transparency

Deliverable:

A stable national digital fruit marketplace.

---

# 5. Phase 2 — Digital Inspection

Primary objectives:

- mobile scanner
- image capture
- inspection reports
- measurement engine
- colour engine
- surface analysis
- Fruit Profile System

Deliverable:

Standardized digital inspection workflows.

---

# 6. Phase 3 — AI Platform

Primary objectives:

- dataset architecture
- AI model lifecycle
- recommendation engine
- demand forecasting
- price intelligence
- marketplace intelligence

Deliverable:

Explainable AI-assisted decision support.

---

# 7. Phase 4 — Dedicated Hardware

Primary objectives:

- handheld scanner

- offline processing

Professional portable inspection devices.

# 8. Phase 5 — Industrial Automation

Primary objectives:

Enterprise-scale inspection infrastructure.

# 9. Phase 6 — Global Expansion

Future opportunities may include:

The roadmap should remain:

- modular

- technology-neutral
