# Data Protection and Privacy

**Project:** Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform (DFIP)

**Platform:** eFruitMandi.live

**Company:** Orchard Growers Private Limited

**Document ID:** DFIP-V08-D25

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-17

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Data Protection and Privacy framework for the Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that personal, commercial, operational, inspection, and AI-related data are collected, processed, stored, shared, and retained in a secure, transparent, and responsible manner.

Data protection applies throughout the complete platform lifecycle.

---

# 2. Objectives

The framework shall:

- protect personal information
- protect commercial information
- secure inspection records
- support user privacy
- enable responsible AI
- support regulatory compliance
- preserve user trust

---

# 3. Data Categories

The platform may manage:

- user profile data
- authentication data
- KYC information
- business information
- Fruit Lot data
- inspection reports
- marketplace transactions
- payment records
- logistics information
- AI datasets
- operational logs
- audit records

Each category should have appropriate protection requirements.

---

# 4. Data Lifecycle

```text
Collection
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Storage
      ↓
Processing
      ↓
Sharing
      ↓
Archival
      ↓
Retention
      ↓
Secure Deletion
```

Each stage should follow approved security and governance policies.

---

# 5. Data Collection

The platform should collect only information necessary for:

- user registration
- marketplace operations
- inspection services
- logistics
- payment processing
- analytics
- platform improvement

Unnecessary data collection should be avoided.

---

# 6. Data Storage

Stored information should support:

- encryption at rest
- redundancy
- integrity validation
- secure backups
- disaster recovery
- version management

Storage technologies may evolve without changing platform policies.

---

# 7. Data Sharing

Data sharing should respect:

- user permissions
- ownership
- visibility rules
- contractual obligations
- platform policies

Only authorized recipients should receive protected information.

---

# 8. Privacy Controls

Users may be provided with controls for:

- profile visibility
- business visibility
- inspection sharing
- notification preferences
- communication preferences
- account management

Privacy settings should be clearly understandable.

---

# 9. Retention

Retention policies may differ by data type.

Retention considerations include:

- legal requirements
- operational needs
- audit requirements
- AI dataset governance
- business continuity

Expired data should be handled according to approved retention policies.

---

# 10. Secure Deletion

Where permitted by policy and law, secure deletion should:

- remove active records
- remove cached copies where practical
- preserve required audit history
- protect platform integrity

Deletion processes should be auditable.

---

# 11. Future Evolution

Future privacy capabilities may include:

- granular consent management
- automated data classification
- privacy dashboards
- secure data portability
- anonymization services
- advanced governance automation

---

# 12. Design Principles

The Data Protection and Privacy framework should remain:

- privacy-aware
- secure
- transparent
- auditable
- scalable
- standards-aligned
- user-centric
- backward compatible
