# Project Vision

**Project:** Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform (DFIP)

**Platform:** eFruitMandi.live

**Company:** Orchard Growers Private Limited

**Document ID:** DFIP-V01-D01

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-17

---

# 1. Purpose

The Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform is a long-term technology initiative designed to improve transparency, trust, standardization, and decision-making across the fruit supply chain.

It will combine:

- digital fruit trading
- structured Fruit Lot data
- live camera-based observation
- fruit size and packing intelligence
- inspection reports
- human verification
- artificial intelligence
- dedicated scanning hardware
- logistics and transaction data

The system will begin with practical browser and mobile-camera tools and gradually evolve into a dedicated fruit-scanning and intelligence ecosystem.

---

# 2. Core Vision

The long-term vision is to create a trusted digital infrastructure through which fruit can be:

- presented accurately
- observed consistently
- measured transparently
- packed according to structured standards
- inspected before trade
- compared by buyers
- verified by humans
- analyzed by artificial intelligence
- linked with marketplace transactions
- tracked through logistics and settlement

The platform should reduce information imbalance between Growers and Buyers without removing human judgment.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Fruit trading commonly depends on:

- verbal descriptions
- limited sample images
- inconsistent grading terms
- non-standard packing descriptions
- physical market inspection
- personal relationships
- manual estimation
- delayed dispute discovery
- buyer and grower interpretation differences

This creates uncertainty around:

- fruit size
- colour
- visible quality
- packing configuration
- package count
- approximate fruit count
- surface condition
- lot consistency
- actual condition at dispatch
- actual condition at delivery

The Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform will create structured digital evidence while preserving the existing authority of Grower-entered commercial data and final human verification.

---

# 4. Grower Vision

The platform should help Growers:

- present Fruit Lots professionally
- record clear images and scan observations
- describe packing accurately
- reduce repeated buyer questions
- establish a digital quality history
- improve buyer confidence
- compare intended grading with observed characteristics
- receive better market visibility
- build a trusted digital business profile
- preserve ownership of their commercial information

The scanner must guide Growers rather than punish or block them unnecessarily.

---

# 5. Buyer Vision

The platform should help Buyers:

- understand Fruit Lots before submitting offers
- compare structured packing information
- review inspection observations
- examine supporting images
- identify uncertainty clearly
- make more informed commercial decisions
- reduce avoidable disputes
- compare Grower-entered information with advisory scan results
- access report versions linked with the transaction

Inspection results must remain advisory unless explicitly verified and accepted under a defined business process.

---

# 6. Marketplace Vision

eFruitMandi.live will act as the commercial layer connecting:

- Growers
- Buyers
- Fruit Lots
- offers
- Deals
- inspection reports
- logistics
- payments
- delivery acceptance
- invoices and challans

The Digital Fruit Intelligence Platform will act as the observation and intelligence layer.

The marketplace and intelligence systems must remain connected but logically separate.

---

# 7. Inspection Vision

A future inspection may contain:

- fruit identity
- variety
- scan mode
- captured frames
- frame-quality observations
- visible fruit candidates
- approximate fruit count
- calibration status
- estimated diameter
- suggested size distribution
- colour observations
- surface observations
- shape observations
- packing observations
- confidence values
- warnings and limitations
- human corrections
- report version
- model version

No inspection result should silently overwrite the Fruit Lot's authoritative commercial fields.

---

# 8. Artificial Intelligence Vision

Artificial intelligence will be introduced gradually.

The intended progression is:

1. deterministic browser-based observation
2. structured metadata collection
3. human correction
4. verified ground-truth creation
5. experimental model development
6. controlled model validation
7. advisory production predictions
8. monitored model improvement
9. hardware-assisted scanning
10. industrial scanning integration

AI must not be presented as accurate before sufficient evidence, validation, and field testing exist.
